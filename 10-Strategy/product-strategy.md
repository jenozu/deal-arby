# Product Strategy

The initial objective is occasional, low-capital online arbitrage rather than Amazon FBA. Start with recognizable, liquid products where exact model matching is feasible and the source price is generally no more than CAD 150.

Current baseline thresholds are stored in `50-Code/config/strategy.yaml`: minimum CAD 25 estimated net profit, 35% ROI, 15% profit margin, and 20% discount to market. Ontario-style 13% purchase tax is the current default example and must eventually become source/location aware.

The engine should prefer conservative quick-sale value over optimistic asking-price value. Counterfeit-prone or ambiguous products should remain manual-review opportunities until matching and authentication evidence is strong enough.
